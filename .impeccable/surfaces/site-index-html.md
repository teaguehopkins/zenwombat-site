# Surface: studio homepage (site/index.html)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: browser-game players arriving from shared links or search, plus career-adjacent visitors judging the hobby. Action: press Play on a game. Proof: the games themselves (LoL key art, a live simulation in Vector's own visual vocabulary). Constraints: brand-only, honest device/length badges, static HTML/CSS + a little JS, no build step. Direction pinned by user: dark arcade-minimal. Build path: code-led (no image generation).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage IS a game-select screen on a vector display. Each game is a menu row you pick with arrow keys or the pointer, and Enter or Play launches it. Refuses the studio-template of a centered headline over a row of equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: Phosphor-on-black vector display. A near-black blue-tinted ground, pale phosphor line ink for rules, the wordmark, and the wombat glyph, drawn as thin strokes with slight bloom. Rows are full-width bands separated by hairlines. The selected row wakes up in its own game's colors: LoL in brown field, gold and the red notification dot; Vector in cyan ink and the four ship colors. Tektur for display, Martian Mono for data, labels and hints.

STORY: A visitor sees a studio with two games, learns each game's hook, run length and devices, and plays one within seconds.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A slim top strip holds the wordmark + wombat glyph on the left and "Small games. Free. In your browser." on the right. Below it are two stacked game rows filling the viewport. Each has a big title, hook, fact chips, and a PLAY control on the left, and art at the right ~55% (LoL key art; Vector live canvas: ships orbiting a gravity well with tracers). A controls bar at the bottom reads "↑↓ choose · Enter play". Signature interaction: keyboard/pointer selection moving a cursor between rows, with the selected row lighting up in its game's palette.

FORM: Game-select menu on a vector display. The user pinned only the visual direction (dark arcade-minimal). The game-select form was the builder's choice, and there's no seed key because no concept-seed roll was made.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
