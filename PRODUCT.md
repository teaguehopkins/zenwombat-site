# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS with no build step, deployed by GitHub Pages at zenwombat.com (confirmed in the approved plan).

## Users

Browser-game players who arrive from a shared link, a game's own page, or search. They want to know in a few seconds what each game is and start playing. A secondary audience is people who see the studio linked from its maker's career materials and judge it as a hobby portfolio.

## Product Purpose

Zen Wombat is a hobby indie studio that makes small, free browser games. The homepage is its front door. Success means visitors click Play on a game. It is not a business: no monetization, no accounts, no newsletter.

## Positioning

A tiny studio whose games each take one familiar thing and treat it with care: a satire of notification overload, a space shooter that takes Newtonian physics seriously. It has no install and no store, and you're playing within seconds.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Games (only these two for now):
  - **Legends of Loot**: https://legendsofloot.zenwombat.com. A satirical game about clearing notifications in the launcher of a fake free-to-play card game. Runs take 5–10 minutes. Works on phone and desktop with mouse, touch, and keyboard. Has a speedrun timer and Zen mode.
  - **Vector: Realistic Space Flight**: https://vector.zenwombat.com. A top-down space arena with momentum physics: 1 player vs 3 AI. Modes are Slingshot (daily challenge with a global leaderboard, plus practice), Survival, Deathmatch, and King of the Hill. Ship customization and upgrades persist between matches. Keyboard/gamepad only, desktop-oriented (it warns mobile visitors).
- Vector is served from Render, and its first load can be slow.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Zen Wombat**. Brand-only credit: no personal name anywhere on the site, and no link to a personal site.
- Each game keeps its own identity. Legends of Loot: warm fantasy brown/gold with a red notification dot. Vector: dark field, flat vector shapes, cyan/red/blue/yellow ship colors.
- Visual direction pinned by the user: dark arcade-minimal.

## Evidence on Hand

- `~/notif-game/public/og.png` is the Legends of Loot 1200×630 key art.
- There are no player counts, reviews, press, or awards. Do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

1. Games first: every screen exists to get someone into a game.
2. Honest expectations: say how long a run takes and what device it needs.
3. Small and calm: no hype, no dark patterns, no monetization language.
